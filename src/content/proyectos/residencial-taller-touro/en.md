Residential-workshop project set in the ruins of Touro (Ribeira, A Coruña). The
buildings do not compete with the landscape: they rise and cluster at different
heights, leaving room for the view and the vegetation through point supports.

They are semi-transparent house-shaped boxes that, in turn, enclose room-like
boxes —a strategy inspired by Sou Fujimoto's Setonomori housing project.

The project combines collage techniques for the composition of the plans in
Illustrator and Photoshop, with drafting managed in AutoCAD and Rhinoceros.
