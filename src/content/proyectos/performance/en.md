This intervention explores the fragility of architecture through a set built with
pomegranates. The fruit — associated with abundance and the passing of time —
becomes an ephemeral material that transforms the space and questions permanence.

The performance, staged at the Madrid School of Architecture (ETSAM), brings the
body, matter and time into dialogue.
