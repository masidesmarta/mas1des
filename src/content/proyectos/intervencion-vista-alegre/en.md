Residential-workshop project located on the Vista Alegre estate, intervening on
some existing sheds. The work focuses on the interior metal skeletons and on the
importance of the staircases as the element of a targeted intervention.

The materials are light and the structures are conceived as a hard-to-define
skeleton, with inclined columns that represent a seed introduced into the
existing building.

Managed entirely in AutoCAD and Rhinoceros.
