Master's Final Project set on the mining landscape of the Riotinto Mines
(Huelva), understanding architecture as a tool for the environmental
regeneration of the place.

The proposal reuses the existing infrastructure and takes advantage of the
site's resources to create new spaces for production, research and gathering.
Through passive solutions, construction systems adapted to the surroundings and
a circular-economy approach, the project reconciles industrial memory,
sustainability and architectural innovation.

At the heart of the intervention, a large market covered by a light textile
structure reorders the cycle of water and materials from the mine, turning the
waste of the extraction process into the material that regenerates the landscape.
