Final project of the BIM Master's, based on the West Glow building (offices and
retail) by Society of Architecture, in South Korea.

From the original drawings, the complete model was built in Revit, with custom
details and element families —walls, windows, railings and staircases. The
exercise made it possible to parametrise and reconstruct a real space through
the BIM system.

Managed entirely in Revit, with images produced in Lumion and Enscape.
