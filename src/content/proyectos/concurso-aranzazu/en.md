Project developed for the Pladur 2025 competition: a workshop with housing in
Arantzazu. The proposal seeks not to become the protagonist nor to compete with
the basilica, but to accompany it.

To that end it reuses the same materials of the surroundings and echoes the
church's pointed forms, creating a path and a controlled view towards the
sanctuary. The exercise was resolved entirely in Revit and laid out in Photoshop.

It received the **Honourable mention** (Accésit) at the Pladur 2025 competition.
